The four agents

Sales Strategy Assistant

Agentforce employee agentAgent Script
  • Briefs the rep with a Claude-written summary grounded in CRM and live telemetry
  • Reads consumption through the Snowflake SQL API
  • Asks Snowflake's Cortex agent ad hoc questions
  • Recommends use cases with peer proof
  • Drafts outreach; the rep sends
  • Routes pricing to Deal Desk, never quotes it

Account Plan Autofill

Record-triggered flowPrompt Builder
  • One click creates the plan
  • Grounds it in Snowflake once, saves the exact data used
  • Claude drafts trends, use cases, landscape, SWOT and vision
  • Fills in section by section, then notifies the owner

Support Intake Agent

Agentforce service agentCortex Search
  • Verifies the customer by email
  • Answers only from knowledge articles in Snowflake
  • Opens routed cases: tier A plus high severity goes to Tier 2
  • Sends pricing questions to the account team
